Matteo Pais (IPA: /matˈtɛ.o ˈpa.i̯s/, listen , roughly “mut-teh-aw pyce”)

I am an Italian computational astrophysicist and postdoctoral researcher at the National Institute for Astrophysics (INAF), at the Astronomical Observatory of Padova, where I work with Dr. Riccardo Ciolfi within the PRIN project EMERGE. I am also a research associate of INFN Padova (Group IV, theory) and a member of the KM3NeT collaboration, the MezzoCielo science team, TEONGRAV and GRAWITA.

Before returning to Italy, I was a postdoctoral fellow at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem (2020–2024) in the group of Prof. Tsvi Piran (ERC Advanced Grants MultiJets and TReX). I did my PhD (2016–2020) at Heidelberg University within the IMPRS-HD, working with Prof. Christoph Pfrommer at the Heidelberg Institute for Theoretical Studies (HITS) and the Leibniz Institute for Astrophysics Potsdam (AIP). I studied physics (BSc and MSc) at the University of Cagliari.

Research in a nutshell

I work on high-energy astrophysics: I combine analytic models with large, GPU-accelerated relativistic magnetohydrodynamic simulations to follow how relativistic jets are born in neutron star mergers and collapsing massive stars, how they break out (or get choked), and what light, neutrinos and cosmic rays they leave behind. [read more]

  • Relativistic jets in BNS mergers & collapsars
  • Shock breakout & GW–EM delay
  • Choked jets & hidden neutrino sources
  • Kilonovae & r-process
  • Cosmic-ray acceleration (SNRs, microquasars)
  • GRB afterglows
  • GPU relativistic MHD (PLUTO / gPLUTO)
