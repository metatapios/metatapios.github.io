About me

A few things about me that have nothing to do with relativistic jets – or almost.

Piano

I play the piano. It is my favourite way to switch off after a day spent debugging simulations – and, like a good simulation, a difficult piece only converges after a lot of patience and many, many iterations.

Swimming

I swim to keep body and mind in shape. Lap after lap is the closest I get to a steady, laminar flow: no shocks, no instabilities – on a good day.

Travel

Research has made me a bit of a nomad: I have lived in Sardinia (Cagliari), Germany (Heidelberg and Potsdam), Israel (Jerusalem) and now in Veneto (Padova), and talked about jets and cosmic rays from Kyoto, Tokyo and Shanghai to Geneva, Copenhagen, Dublin and Chania. I love discovering new places, their food and their languages – I speak Italian, English, German, some French and Spanish, and a little Hebrew and Japanese.

Travel highlight so far: winning a Pochita plushie at a crane game in Shibuya, Tokyo – on my very first try.

Fuel

Science, code and anything else that requires a high dose of caffeinated beverages.
